# MacroBay 0.4.5

This page covers what someone using the app will notice, and what is worth reading before upgrading an
existing copy. For what the app can and cannot do overall, see
[Status and limitations](../status-and-limitations.md).

## Download

- macOS · Apple silicon: `MacroBay-arm64.dmg`
- macOS · Intel: `MacroBay-x64.dmg`
- Windows: the installer ships with this version

**None of the three packages has completed a first-install run on real hardware**: arm64, Intel and
Windows. They build and ship, but there is no record of an actual first install.

Builds are unsigned and not notarised, so the first launch still needs the workaround in item 1 below.

## Before you upgrade

1. **This version is neither code-signed nor notarised.** On macOS, drag the app into Applications,
   then **right-click (or Control-click) → Open** and confirm — a plain double-click is stopped by
   Gatekeeper. When the right-click menu has no "Open" (newer macOS), go to **System Settings →
   Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. On Windows, run the installer and choose **More
   info → Run anyway** in SmartScreen. **Both steps repeat after every update you download.**
2. **Calendar reminders will fire more often than before, on purpose.** Sources used to label almost
   everything as medium, so the default "high only" setting never triggered; this version grades events
   itself by release family, so **CPI, non-farm payrolls and FOMC will actually alert**. If you turned
   reminders on earlier and left the level at its default, check it after upgrading.
3. **The US Bureau of Labor Statistics calendar is still unreachable (403 from the source)**; automatic
   sync stays off and the events already in the app remain at the last successful sync.
4. **Two platform-side switches are still prerequisites.** Discord needs **Message Content** enabled
   for the bot in the developer console; Slack needs **Socket Mode** enabled in the app itself.

## What you will see

### Dashboard: a daily Treasury yield curve

- **A new "Treasury yields · daily" card** with a readout table on the left and a curve on the right:
  five tenors (3M to 30Y), two spreads, and the 10Y split into real yield and inflation compensation.
- **Two ways to read the curve**: "yield" for absolute levels, "indexed to start" for the move in basis
  points from the window's start; several window lengths are available.
- **The series behind it grew from one to 19** (11 nominal, 5 real, 3 inflation expectation), which is
  what lets it answer whether a move is real yields or inflation expectations.
- The data is the FRED daily close and **may lag one to two trading days**; the card says so.

### Dashboard: fear and downside protection on one card

- **New card, "Fear & Greed and Downside Premium"**: a gauge in the middle, a trend on each side, and
  the combined reading on its own line below.
- **It states the situation only** — no direction, no entry or exit.
- **The halves are not comparable**: a global crypto sentiment index against the downside premium
  priced into BTC options on Deribit alone. They are not added up and do not confirm each other; both
  dates are the source's own date.
- **The options half has no proxy source yet and reads "Not connected"** — not "no data". It draws no
  line and is not an empty state; numbers appear once a proxy is connected.
- **Both sources appear under Settings → Sources** (Fear & Greed, options skew): capabilities, switches
  and status are there, and the skew source reads "Not connected".

### Dashboard: charts are easier to read, and harder to misread

- **A new palette for the light theme**: five colours were too faint on white and some lines were
  nearly invisible; all five now read clearly.
- **Hover panels no longer cover the column you are reading**: at the right edge the panel flips to the
  left of the cursor.
- **One vertical scrollbar on the dashboard**, not two.
- **The positioning chart is drawn taller**: the plot uses the height the card can give it (previously
  only half).
- **Text no longer shrinks when the card narrows**: the chart is redrawn from the container's pixels,
  and the labels keep their size.
- **Liquidation markers are split by direction**: longs and shorts each get their own row and colour
  instead of one "suspected liquidation ×N" — a long liquidation and a short liquidation mean opposite
  things.
- **Tighter time-axis labels on the funding-rate chart**: labels on the same day show the time only.
- **The same-start yield axis no longer repeats the time zone on every tick.**
- **Cross-asset reading no longer says "undetermined" whenever one leg is missing**: it states what the
  data on hand rules out, and what it cannot rule out.
- **Rows for unavailable sources no longer carry show/hide switches**: the switches were removed for a
  fixed rule, so which sources appear is no longer something you have to toggle.

### Funds: dates are trading days

- **"Latest" and "streak" read as "N trading days"**, weekends and provisional days excluded.
- **The as-of row is the data's own last day**, not today; a date sitting on the previous trading day
  is normal.
- **The ETF row aligns to trading days too**, rather than treating today as the data date.
- **When one cell is not enabled, the sentence names which source is off**, instead of leaving you to
  guess which feed is quiet.
- **A failed re-read no longer turns the whole card into an error**: the previous reading stays on
  screen, marked as lagging — no data is lost, it is still the last reading. Only a first read that
  fails reports an error.

### Calendar: grading and event detail

- **Event grading no longer copies the source**: sources labelled nearly everything medium; this
  version grades by release family itself, and events it cannot grade are marked ungraded rather than
  treated as high or low.
- **"AI reading" now appears in the event detail**: title, body, and a line naming when it was generated
  and what it was based on.
- **When it cannot be read, there is one sentence and a small retry that does not span the column.**
- **One row per release**: revision markers are no longer mistaken for a separate period, so the same
  release no longer shows up twice on the same day.

### News: more on one screen

- **The news list shows 30 items per screen and loads 30 more when you reach the bottom** (was 15).

### Notification centre

- **External actions such as "Read original" are now real buttons**, opened in the browser; before,
  the record carried the action but nothing was drawn.
- **Records written in one language no longer become "You have a new notification"** in the other
  language's interface.
- **Action buttons carry their row's title in the accessible name**, so screen readers can tell several
  "Open task" buttons apart.

### Daily briefing and citations

- **When the poster for an automatic push cannot be produced, the text still goes out**: a day is never
  lost because the image failed.
- **A citation is no longer attached three times to one sentence.**

### Sources and settings

- **Raw records have a retention period**: originals and metadata no longer grow without limit.
- **Wide tables use the full-width dialog and scroll horizontally inside it**, instead of being
  squeezed.

## Known limitations

- **None of the three packages — arm64, Intel, Windows — has completed first-install verification on
  real hardware.**
- **The dollar index may have no data at all; that is a plan limit, not a fault.** After subscribing to
  a plan that includes it, the 24-hour window takes about a day to fill and the earlier gap is not
  backfilled.
- **The US Bureau of Labor Statistics calendar is unreachable** (403); see
  [Status and limitations](../status-and-limitations.md).
- **Feishu and QQ send text only in this version**; image egress is open on Telegram, Slack and Discord.
- **The options half of "Fear & Greed and Downside Premium" reads "Not connected" until a proxy
  exists**, draws no line, and stays out of the combined reading.
- **Calendar events graded as ungraded do not alert**: they are neither high nor low, so the default
  "high only" setting will not push them.
- **No auto-update channel**: the app tells you a newer version exists; downloading and installing is
  still yours to do.
- **One alert per item, no second push on a grade change.**

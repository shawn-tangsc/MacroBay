Federal Reserve issues FOMC statement

July 29, 2026

For release at 2:00 p.m. EDT

The Federal Open Market Committee approved the following statement for release:

The Committee decided to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 3-1/2 to 3-3/4 percent, in support of its goals.

The Committee is continuing its policy of reducing its holdings of Treasury securities and agency debt.

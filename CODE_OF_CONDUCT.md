# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, technical, and respectful. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, personal attacks, and
discrimination are not.

Examples of expected behavior:

- critique the change, not the person;
- explain trade-offs and evidence;
- assume good faith until proven otherwise;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's data or credentials.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- insults, threats, or sustained disruption;
- discriminatory language;
- publishing private information without permission;
- sharing credentials, local databases, or personal screenshots.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block participants who violate this
policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and explained when practical.

## Reporting

Report a conduct issue privately to the maintainers through the repository profile. Do not open a
public issue that contains personal or sensitive details.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific policy, not a legal document.
